Privacy Policy

Your notes stay yours.

Toddi is designed to keep your mood entries private on your device.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Data Toddi stores

Toddi stores the mood, optional note, and dates associated with each check-in in the app's local storage on your device.

Data collection and tracking

Toddi does not create an account, collect analytics, show advertising, track you across apps or websites, or send your mood entries and notes to a server operated by Toddi.

Apple and the App Store may process download, diagnostic, or device information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Toddi does not receive your private check-in content from Apple.

Your control

You can edit or delete individual entries in the app. You can also delete every check-in from History → More → About Toddi.

Deletion in Toddi cannot remove copies that may already exist in device backups managed by Apple and your device settings. Removing the app removes its local data from that device, subject to those backups.

Children and health use

Toddi is a general reflection tool intended for adults. It is not a medical device or healthcare service and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, crisis, or emergency support.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its date. Questions about this policy can be sent to:
