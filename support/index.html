Entries are missing after reinstalling

Toddi stores entries locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app can remove local data. Availability in a device backup depends on your Apple backup settings.

A character video is not playing

Toddi shows a static character when Reduce Motion is enabled or a video asset is unavailable. You can review this in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion.
